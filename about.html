Our Story

A Decade of Beauty Excellence

"Beauty is about being comfortable in your own skin."

Founded in 2014, SOLO Salon began as a small boutique studio with a big dream — to redefine the salon experience. What started as a passion project has grown into one of the most trusted names in beauty and hair care.

Our founder, with over 15 years of international experience, envisioned a space where artistry meets technique, where every client is treated as an individual, and where the latest global trends are brought to life with precision and care.

Today, SOLO Salon is home to a team of 15+ skilled professionals, each bringing their unique expertise to deliver transformations that go beyond the surface — boosting confidence and celebrating individuality.
